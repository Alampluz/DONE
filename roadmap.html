3What's next

Ordered by what the team actually runs into. Each one starts only when the previous is accepted.

1

Make ownership imply visibility — fixed 4 Sep

Verified 1 Sep: a non-admin owner can delete a board they own, cannot delete the workspace it sits in, cannot touch a board they do not own, and a requester cannot hold ownership at all. The model works — with one hole. Postgres applies SELECT policies to any delete whose WHERE reads a column, and projects_select is membership-based, so an owner who is not a member of the board or workspace cannot see it: the delete matches nothing and raises no error, and the UI simply does nothing. Nobody is affected today, but the member dialogs can hand ownership to a non-member.

Accepted 4 Sep: projects_select now admits is_board_owner(id) and is_ws_owner(workspace_id); probed before and after in rolled-back transactions.
2

Notifications that leave the app — built 4 Sep, waiting on IT

Every notification now queues for email through Microsoft 365 (workos@crea.asia) with per-person mutes. The one thing left is credentials: an Entra app with Mail.Send scoped to that mailbox and five Supabase secrets, listed in docs_email_setup.md. Email-to-task (inbound) is the follow-on once mail is flowing.

Accepts when: the queued test message reaches April's inbox, then a real mention and an SLA breach each arrive within a minute.
3

Per-board permission presets — shipped 4 Sep

Three presets per board, enforced in the database and mirrored in the UI. What remains from the original ask is the granular per-action matrix; the preset functions (can_write_board, can_edit_task) are where it would grow if a real case appears.

Accepted: a view-only member is refused an edit by the database, not just by a hidden button, and test_presets.cjs covers each preset.
4

Somewhere safe to break things

There is no test environment: sandbox.html is the live database wearing an orange bar. Supabase branching needs the Pro plan, so the route is a second free project replayed from supabase_migrations.schema_migrations, seeded with its own auth users.

Accepts when: the schema rebuilds from migration history alone, someone can log in, and a destructive test there leaves production untouched.
5

Operational hygiene

New 6 Sep: seven CS logins to create (list in the session notes) and then the assignee backfill on the imported board; three pre-import test rows on CS Inquiries to archive. Then the standing items: five root-level files were truncated to zero bytes in the same minute on 31 August with no write issued to four of them; the cause is still unidentified. That deserves an off-folder copy of the handoff and runners. Alongside it: turn on Supabase leaked-password protection, and exercise the platform and file column types on one real board before a team meets them cold.

Accepts when: losing the WorkOS folder costs nothing that matters, and both unused column types have been used in anger at least once.

4Still deliberately not built

Cut is not rejected — each returns when real usage produces the problem it solves.

ProposedPosition
12-status workflowStill five statuses plus the SLA pause. Twelve states on a small team becomes theatre; people stop updating them and every report goes wrong.
Seven roles (Department Head, Manager, Team Lead)The existing six, now joined by ownership and teams — which is what those extra roles were reaching for. Each additional role multiplies every permission test.
Field-level permissionsStill heavy machinery with no incident driving it. Board presets (item 3 above) are the cheaper answer to the same complaint.
Gantt / Timeline, capacity forecastingP2. Per-board Workload already answers who is overloaded.
Time tracking & estimatesTurnaround is measured from history automatically. Manual timers go unused unless billing depends on them.
Stripe billing, custom domain, admin_fn_v5 edge functionOnly worth doing if WorkOS is offered outside CREA. Nothing internal is waiting on them.
